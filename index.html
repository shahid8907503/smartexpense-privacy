Privacy Policy for Smart Expense Pro

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Developer: S. Shahid Farwez

Welcome to Smart Expense Pro. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details what information we handle, how it is processed, and your rights. In short: your financial transactions, budgets, recurring bills, split bills, and savings goals remain private and stored strictly on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Financial & Transaction Data: Amounts, transaction categories, dates, descriptions, payment modes, recurring bills, debt/IOU records, and savings goals that you enter manually, by voice, or by parsing. All of this data is stored in a private, encrypted local SQLite/Room database on your device. We do not operate an external server collecting your transaction history.
  • Audio / Microphone Data: When you use the Intelligent Voice Entry or Voice Assistant feature, audio is processed on-device (via Android's built-in Google Speech Recognition API) solely to transcribe your speech into text (e.g., "Spent 250 on lunch"). We do not record, store, or transmit your voice recordings to any third-party server.
  • Biometric Data: If you enable the Biometric App Lock feature, authentication is handled natively by the Android OS BiometricPrompt framework. We never access, store, or transmit your fingerprint, face, or PIN data.
  • Shared Text / Clipboard Data: If you use the Smart Bank & UPI Parser (via paste or the Android Share sheet), the message text is processed 100% locally on your device to extract transaction details. No message content is ever uploaded anywhere.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

Smart Expense Pro uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information to serve ads in accordance with Google's policies:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • General coarse location data (IP-based)
  • App performance and diagnostic data

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Storage, Security, and Protection

  • All personal settings and critical credentials utilize Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences and internal sandboxed application storage.
  • The application enforces strict TLS/HTTPS transport (cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled).
  • An optional Anti-Screenshot & Screen Recording privacy shield (FLAG_SECURE) can be enabled to prevent external apps or OS snapshots from capturing your financial data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your financial data resides solely on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • You can clear any single transaction, bill, goal, or split record inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage via your device settings permanently deletes all stored records.
  • You can export your complete data anytime using the built-in Export CSV feature.

5. Children's Privacy

Smart Expense Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
